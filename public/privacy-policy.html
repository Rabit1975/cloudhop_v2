Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 26, 2026

1. Introduction

CloudHop ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account registration information (email, username, profile data)
  • Google Account information when you sign in with Google
  • Content you upload or create (playlists, preferences, gameplay data)
  • Communications you send us

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (OS, browser type, IP address)
  • Usage data (pages visited, games played, features used)
  • Cookie and tracking data
  • Analytics data (via Google Analytics)

2.3 Third-Party Services

When you authenticate via Google Sign-In, we receive:

  • Email address
  • Profile name and picture
  • Linked accounts (YouTube, etc.)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts
  • Personalize your experience
  • Send you updates and announcements
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and secure our services
  • Analyze usage patterns and optimize performance

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers (hosting, analytics, payments)

  • Your explicit consent

4.1 Third-Party Services

10. Google API Services & YouTube Data

CloudHop's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

10.1 Why We Use Google Sign-In

CloudHop uses Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0 via accounts.google.com) only to authenticate users who wish to access the YouTube Music feature of the platform. Users who do not use the Music feature are never prompted for Google authentication.

10.2 Scopes Requested and Their Purpose

  • openid — Required by the OpenID Connect standard to securely verify your identity and issue a signed token. This scope does not expose any additional personal data beyond what is listed below.
  • email — Your Google email address is used as the unique identifier for your CloudHop session. It allows us to recognise returning users, link your music preferences to your account, and prevent duplicate or conflicting sign-ins. We do not use your email for marketing without your explicit consent.
  • profile — Your display name and profile photo are shown within the Music feature interface so you can confirm which Google account is currently active. This data is not stored in our database beyond the browser session.

10.3 Data We Do NOT Collect via Google

  • We do not access your YouTube watch history, liked videos, subscriptions, or playlists.
  • We do not post, upload, delete, or modify any content on your YouTube or Google account.
  • We do not request Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google service data.
  • We do not store your Google OAuth tokens in our database.
  • We do not share Google account data with any third party for advertising or analytics.

10.4 Limited Use Compliance

CloudHop's use of data obtained through Google APIs is limited to the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Specifically:

  • Data obtained via Google APIs is used only to provide or improve the YouTube Music feature to the authenticated user.
  • We do not use Google API data to serve advertisements.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google account data unless you have given explicit permission or it is required for security investigations.
  • We do not transfer Google API data to third parties except as necessary to provide the in-app Music feature, and only with your permission.

10.5 Revoking Google Access

You can revoke CloudHop's access to your Google account at any time by visiting https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and removing CloudHop from the list of apps with access. This will immediately sign you out of the YouTube Music feature. All locally held session data is cleared when you log out.
