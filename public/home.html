How We Use Your Google Account Data

Why CloudHop requests Google Sign-In

CloudHop uses Google OAuth (Sign In With Google) exclusively to authenticate users who want to access the YouTube Music feature. We request only the minimum scopes required:

  • openid — Required by OpenID Connect to verify your identity securely.
  • email — Used to identify your CloudHop account and prevent duplicate sign-ins.
  • profile — Your display name and profile picture are shown inside the Music player UI so you know which Google account is active.

We do not request write access to your YouTube account, do not access your watch history, do not store your Google credentials, and do not share your Google data with any third parties. Your Google account data is used only during the authenticated session and is never sold. For full details see our Privacy Policy.
